How a restock product is read

Two reads, one join, one ledger.

  1. The demand is FeedHero’s Ads Traffic report for this market over the trailing 30 days — one row per product Google Ads served, keyed on the client’s own product ID. Only products with an impression are listed, so a feed product absent from the whole read had none.
  2. The stock state is the output feed as it is right now, streamed here product by product: in stock, pre-order or backorder (buyable), out of stock, or no availability word at all (not stated — its own bucket, never read either way).
  3. The join is the product ID, case-blind (Google lower-cases the Ads item id that stands in for a blank one). A demanded product the feed does not carry is Not in feed — FeedHero’s own “Unlisted SKUs in Ads traffic”.
  4. Days unavailable come from a ledger this page keeps per market — the day a product was first seen out of stock or gone — never from the window: 30 days of demand says nothing about when the stock ran out. Tracking begins the first time a market is opened here and says so.
  5. Money never crosses a currency. Every sum is one currency’s; a read FeedHero returns in two currencies sums impressions, clicks and conversions only, and the page says why.
