Quote generator

Scope an AI field-generation engagement off the client's live feed. The six Google conversational attributes sit on top; below them is the full Tachyon optimisation catalogue (titles, descriptions, highlights, keywords, attributes, GPC/PT) — the same library as the Pricer, grouped and collapsible. What are you quoting?

Pick every part of the engagement this quote covers. Each type gets its own section below and its own place on the Excel export; the totals and the tracker combine them. Every £ is a rate on the shared rate card — change it and it saves for every client.

New system — FeedHero dashboard

A system is a FeedHero dashboard for one market — a brand with several markets needs one per market — charged per system per month on the Monthly side of the quote. Tick the markets (the brand's wired markets are listed; + add any other). The strip below holds the monthly rate and the automatic setup hours — taken from the retainer, or charged one-off at the day rate.

New feed

Three kinds of feed, every one a monthly charge per feed (ex VAT) on the Monthly side of the quote. A generic feed is a fixed £ per feed per month. A bespoke feed depends on its complexity: capture the requirements and they go to ASPL for costing — the £ stays blank on the quote until it is agreed. A local inventory feed has its own monthly rate. Setup hours are added automatically per feed, whatever the kind — taken from the retainer, or charged one-off at the day rate.

Retainer hours

One-off hours and / or hours per month at the day rate (the block rate in Settings — £595 per 8 hours). One-off hours land in the one-off total; monthly hours in the ManPower (Days / Month) section of the export.

Product scope

The base volume every ticked field is quoted against — counted in variant SKUs (every SKU row in the live feed — the product scope is always SKU level). Pick a headline number, choose exact product types from the live feed, or cover the whole catalogue. Each field can be overridden below.

Fields to generate

Tick an optimisation to price it. The six conversational attributes are on top; the Tachyon catalogue follows, grouped — click a group header to fold it. Rates seed from the catalogue (your Pricer figures) and save to your rate card; type over any to override for this client. Next to the charge, the generation method — manual, rules, predefined rules or AI + script — records how the team will produce the field, so ASPL sizes the work from the quote (it labels the line on the export, the email and the brief; the £ and hours stay yours per line). Every line carries its own note (it lands in the quote's Additional Notes, the email and the brief) and is charged £ per SKU, as a one-off (hours at the block rate) or monthly (hours per month — a recurring line). A per-SKU or one-off line that also needs looking after takes maintenance hours per month in its own box. Everything monthly builds the Monthly column of the summary below.

How the numbers work

Volumes are pulled live from the client's Google feed and counted in variant SKUs — every SKU row in the feed, per product type (the product scope is always SKU level). Nothing is estimated. £ rates seed from the Tachyon catalogue (the same figures the Pricer uses) and save to your rate card; they are never invented and you can change any. The quote stays hidden until you reveal it, so you can screen-share the scope safely. The full hourly/retainer commercial model lives in the Pricer (▦ apps icon).
