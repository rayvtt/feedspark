Where each figure comes from
  1. The product — title, brand, price, the sale price and the pictures — is a sample of the live output feed, read through the same field summoner the Overlay design studio uses, so a price pair is resolved by value, not by column name.
  2. Clicks and conversions over 30 days are FeedHero’s Google Ads report, product by product — the only per-product demand the FCC reads today. A Meta catalogue market reads its Google market’s report.
  3. Views in the last 24 hours, the weather and the shopper’s audience are not read by the FCC. They are typed here for the preview and marked demo / simulated. In production they come from the Meta pixel (ViewContent) or GA4, a weather source per market, and the network’s own retargeting audiences.
  4. Headline and primary text map onto Meta’s own catalogue keywords where Meta has one ({{product.name}}, {{product.current_price}} …); any other field is written into the feed by a FeedHero rule on the schedule and lands at the next catalogue fetch.
  5. The image tagline is a text parameter on FeedSpark’s image-creator URL — the same engine the Overlays module reads off live feeds — re-rendered whenever the schedule changes what it says.

Safe zones and text limits are the commonly published figures, a guide to confirm in each network’s own Ads Manager preview — the networks move them.
