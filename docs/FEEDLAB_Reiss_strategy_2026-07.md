# Feed Lab — Expert Recommendation Layer
## Reiss UK · Live Google Sheet feed · 19,447 SKUs · Daily refresh
*Strategy narrative — sits above the automated audit. Numbers cited are from the live profile pull.*

---

## 1. Executive read

Reiss UK sits at the **top of Structured, roughly 40% into Enriched, and 0% Agentic-ready**. The foundations are genuinely elite — gtin, brand, colour, size, material, item_group_id, GPC, availability and price all at 100% across 19,447 SKUs, GPC paths averaging 3.6 levels deep, and a FeedHero title pipeline already live in the feed with before/after columns exposed. What's missing is text depth and the 2026 agentic layer: 7,261 titles (37%) under 50 characters against an 80–120 MASK target, 36% of SKUs with no description at all and 10,833 (56%) under 160 characters, and **zero of the six Google 2026 conversational attributes**. The headline move is a **single Tachyon enrichment pass with three payloads** — full-length titles, 300+ character descriptions, and a conversational-attributes supplemental feed generated from the same run. One pipeline, one QA cycle, and the feed jumps two rungs: Enriched closed, Agentic opened, first-mover in UK premium fashion. Everything below is sequencing detail on that move.

## 2. Prioritised recommendations

### R1 — Title lengthening to MASK band (80–120 chars)
- **What:** Rewrite the 7,261 titles (37%) currently under 50 chars; feed avg is 53 vs the 80–120 optimum. Structure: Brand + Material + Fit + Colour + Use-case. Resolve the 132 duplicate titles in the same pass.
- **Why:** Title is the highest-weighted text field in Shopping ranking and the primary string agentic surfaces quote back to users. Sub-50-char titles ("Reiss Navy Merino Wool Crew-neck Jumper, XS") carry no fit or use-case tokens — dead query space on ~37% of the catalogue.
- **Service:** Tachyon AI title rewrite → published via FeedHero rules. The pipeline is **already live** (`c:base_title` / `c:auto_optimised_title` / `c:new_product_name_with_rules` columns in-feed), so before/after measurement is free and rollback is instant.
- **Effort:** M · **Impact:** H
- **Pitch:** *"A third of your catalogue is invisible to queries it should own — the rewrite pipeline is already wired into your feed, we just need to open the tap."*

### R2 — Description coverage and depth
- **What:** Fill the ~7,000 empty descriptions (36% of SKUs) and extend the 10,833 under 160 chars (56%) to 300–500 chars with benefit-led, attribute-dense copy. Feed avg today: 174 chars.
- **Why:** Descriptions are the grounding text LLM surfaces (AI Mode, ChatGPT Shopping, Perplexity) reason over. A blank or 160-char description gives the model nothing to rank, match or answer with. YuMOVE benefit-copy testing delivered +24.7% on this exact lever.
- **Service:** Tachyon AI description enrichment (single pass shared with R1/R3).
- **Effort:** M · **Impact:** H
- **Pitch:** *"36% of your products say nothing about themselves — in an AI shopping answer, silence means exclusion."*

### R3 — Conversational-attributes supplemental feed (Google 2026 spec)
- **What:** Stand up a supplemental data source carrying all six 2026 attributes — question_and_answer, document_link, related_product, item_group_title, variant_option, popularity_rank. Current coverage: 0% on all six.
- **Why:** These are the fields Google's conversational/AI Mode surfaces read directly. Supplemental delivery means zero risk to the live primary feed. Two are near-free: popularity_rank seeds from the existing "Best Sellers" label (43% coverage day one); variant_option maps from size/colour already at 100%.
- **Service:** Conversational-attributes supplemental feed, content generated by the Tachyon pass (Q&A pairs from enriched descriptions), delivered via FeedHero.
- **Effort:** M · **Impact:** H
- **Pitch:** *"Google published the fields its AI answers read from — nobody in your competitive set is filling them yet."*

### R4 — product_highlight build-out to 4–6 slots
- **What:** Slot 1 is at 100% but coverage collapses to 42/41/23% by slots 2–4 and single digits beyond. Target: minimum 4 populated highlights on 100% of SKUs (Google optimum 4–6).
- **Why:** Highlights are scannable bullets used in Shopping detail panels and lifted into AI answer summaries; a single highlight per product wastes the slot architecture already in place.
- **Service:** Tachyon AI — auto-derive highlights from enriched descriptions and imagery.
- **Effort:** S · **Impact:** M/H
- **Pitch:** *"You're using one bullet of a six-bullet allowance on 60% of products."*

### R5 — Custom label architecture rebuild
- **What:** label_0 is 43% filled with a single value ("Best Sellers") and label_1 is 5% — two of five slots effectively dead. label_2 (gender/FP-sale, ~99%), label_3 (stock include/exclude) and label_4 (Incubator) work. Rebuild 0/1 as price-band and margin/newness cohorts.
- **Why:** Custom labels are the campaign-structure control surface for PMax bid segmentation; empty labels mean spend cannot be shaped by profitability or lifecycle.
- **Service:** FeedHero rules & custom labels.
- **Effort:** S · **Impact:** M/H
- **Pitch:** *"Two of your five bid-control levers are unplugged — reconnecting them costs rules, not media."*

### R6 — product_type taxonomy deepening
- **What:** The feed has 10-level capacity but only 8% of SKUs reach depth ≥3. Build product_type to a consistent 3–4 levels, cross-referencing the strong GPC paths (avg depth 3.6) already in place.
- **Why:** product_type is the merchant's own taxonomy signal — it drives campaign subdivision and helps agents disambiguate category intent ("wool overshirt vs jacket"). At 8% depth-3, PMax listing groups are being built on a stub.
- **Service:** FeedHero rules (derive from GPC + title tokens) with Tachyon fallback for ambiguous lines.
- **Effort:** S/M · **Impact:** M
- **Pitch:** *"Your own category tree is one level deep on 92% of products — Google's view of your range is sharper than yours."*

### R7 — Pattern completion via visual attribute harvest
- **What:** pattern is 34% filled. Harvest pattern (plus texture/neckline/sleeve candidates) from product imagery — up to 10 additional image slots per SKU available as source material.
- **Why:** Pattern is a filterable attribute in Shopping and a natural-language hook in agent queries ("striped linen shirt"). 66% of the catalogue can't match those queries on attribute today.

### R8 — short_description layer for Meta + snippet surfaces

- **Why:** Duplicate titles split impression data, muddy A/B reads on the title pipeline, and risk agents treating distinct SKUs as one product.

- **Effort:** S · **Impact:** L/M

## 3. The agentic-commerce angle

**How the surfaces consume this feed.** Google AI Mode reads Merchant Center directly — primary feed plus the 2026 conversational attributes — under **UCP**; ChatGPT Shopping ingests product data via **ACP** and ranks on text richness and answerability; Perplexity resolves products through Shopping-Graph-style entity matching (**MCP**-pattern integrations) and quotes description text in answers. All three share one behaviour: **they are text-first readers**. Structured completeness gets a product into the index; text depth decides whether it appears in the answer.

**Where Reiss wins today:** gtin/brand/colour/size/material at 100% means entity resolution — the hard part for most fashion feeds — is solved. Every surface can identify the product.

**Where the gaps bite, surface by surface:**
- **question_and_answer at 0%** — when a shopper asks ChatGPT or AI Mode "is this jumper machine-washable?", there is no feed-supplied answer; the model guesses from thin text or omits the product. This is the single most direct agentic gap.
- **Descriptions 36% empty / 56% under 160 chars** — LLM rankers ground on copy. A blank description is a product the model cannot argue for; it loses to a competitor SKU with 400 characters of benefit copy, at identical price.
- **variant_option / item_group_title at 0%** — agents can't reason across the variant set ("does this come in navy in a medium?") even though item_group_id is 100%. The relational data exists; the agent-readable expression of it doesn't.
- **popularity_rank at 0%** — assistants over-weight bestseller signals when disambiguating similar items; Reiss holds this data (the "Best Sellers" label proves it) and isn't declaring it.
- **product_type depth ≥3 on only 8%** — category-intent queries ("smart-casual wool overshirt") resolve through taxonomy; a shallow tree pushes disambiguation onto title text that (per R1) is too short to carry it.
- **No short_description** — snippet-length surfaces truncate mid-sentence instead of reading a crafted line.

**Net position:** Reiss is fully machine-*identifiable* but only partially machine-*recommendable*. R1–R3 close exactly that gap, and a supplemental-feed delivery route means the agentic layer ships without touching the live primary feed.

## 4. Three quick wins (inside the normal monthly retainer)

1. **Seed popularity_rank + variant_option in a supplemental feed** — popularity_rank from the existing "Best Sellers" label (43% coverage day one); variant_option mapped from size/colour already at 100%. First agentic attributes live within the month, zero net-new content required.
2. **FeedHero rule pass on the 7,261 short titles** — append fit/use-case tokens from existing attribute columns toward the 80-char floor, and resolve the 132 duplicates. The before/after columns are already in the feed, so the A/B read is free.
3. **product_highlight top-up to 4 slots** — Tachyon-derive highlights from the 64% of SKUs that already hold descriptions, lifting slot-4 coverage from 23% towards 100% on that cohort. Small effort, visible in Shopping panels immediately.
