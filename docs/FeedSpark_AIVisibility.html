How it is measured

Each surface is asked through its own API with its web search on.

SurfaceRead throughModelSwitched on byState

Answers through an API are close to, not identical with, what a signed-in shopper sees in each app: the apps add their own instructions, memory and personalisation. Google AI Mode and AI Overviews are read from Google's own results page, so they are the closest to what a shopper sees. Every surface is asked as a shopper in the market's country, with the date. Visibility counts only unbranded questions; share of voice counts each tracked brand once per answer; position is the order the brand is first named among the tracked brands. A surface that showed no AI answer, or failed, is left out of every rate and counted on its own.
