Product scope

The base volume every ticked field is quoted against — counted in variant SKUs (every SKU row in the live feed — the product scope is always SKU level). Pick a headline number, choose exact product types from the live feed, or cover the whole catalogue. Each field can be overridden below.

Fields to generate

Monthly update — new products

Key message: every new product is updated the month it lands — no waiting for a quarterly catch-up. Priced as a monthly bundle by expected new SKUs per month (minimum £100 a month), sized from the feed's own arrivals history so the forecast pulls straight into the quote.

New system — FeedHero dashboard

A system is a FeedHero dashboard for one market; a brand with several markets needs one per market. Tick the markets below (the brand's wired markets are listed; add any other), at the per-system rate.

New feed

A generic feed is a fixed price per feed (ex VAT). A bespoke feed depends on its complexity: capture the requirements here and they go to ASPL for costing — the price is decided later and stays blank on the quote until it is agreed.

Local inventory feed

A monthly charge per local inventory feed. Tick the markets that need one.

Retainer hours

One-off hours and / or hours per month at the day rate (the block rate in Settings — £595 per 8 hours). One-off hours land in the one-off total; monthly hours land in the ManPower (Days / Month) section of the export.

How the numbers work

Volumes are pulled live from the client's Google feed and counted in variant SKUs — every SKU row in the feed, per product type (the product scope is always SKU level). Nothing is estimated. £ rates seed from the Tachyon catalogue (the same figures the Pricer uses) and save to your rate card; they are never invented and you can change any. The quote stays hidden until you reveal it, so you can screen-share the scope safely. The full hourly/retainer commercial model lives in the Pricer (▦ apps icon).
