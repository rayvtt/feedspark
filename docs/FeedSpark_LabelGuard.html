Custom-label monitoring — every Shopping feed

Live capture of g:custom_label_0–4 across every wired client feed, per market: which values exist, how many SKUs each carries, and how that compares to the last known-good baseline. PMAX listing groups are keyed on these exact values — when a label silently drops out of a feed, six-figure campaigns break with no warning. The hourly sweep re-checks the whole estate and flags any drop-off here and on every FCC page before it reaches campaign performance. A flagged change that was intentional? Open the feed and hit “Expected — rebaseline”.

Alert routing — custom watches

Pin the exact label values PMAX depends on and pick who gets pinged. The worker re-checks every watch live each hour (on the half-hour) — a watched value dropping off fires a high-priority ping to Google Chat, Slack, or email the moment it's seen, re-pings daily while broken, and sends a ✅ when it recovers. Create watches with the 🔔 buttons in the dissection above — on a label card (all its values) or on the cross panel (a breakdown like CL0 “Best Sellers” → CL2).
