Principles

Six rules every screen is judged against.

Clean white, orange with purposeWhite surfaces, charcoal text, orange only where it means something: the primary action, the current thing, a warning.
Data first, honestlySay where a figure came from and when. Never read as zero: show "not scanned" or "not read yet" instead.
One idea per surfaceA card answers one question. One modal at a time. If it needs a paragraph, it needs a pop-up.
Same on the phoneEverything the desktop shows is reachable at 390px. Wide things pan inside their frame; nothing is hidden.
Dark mode is a first-class themeEvery colour is a token with a dark value. No light islands.
Plain EnglishShort sentences an AM can read aloud to a client. A second sentence collapses behind ⓘ.

Charts

Two series colours, one axis per chart.

  • Palette: blue --chart-1 and orange --chart-2 — validated in both themes.
  • Never a dual axis. Two measures = two panels on one calendar.
  • Walk the calendar. A month with nothing is a zero in its own slot, never a gap that closes up.
  • The running month is partial: drawn at .45 and labelled, never plotted as finished.
  • Say what the bars are under the chart, and offer a ⊞ table view.

Layout & chrome

What every page shares, and what it must not rebuild.

  • Topbar: the wordmark, the page tag, then the module row — every module as an icon, right-aligned, identical on every page.
  • Column: 1320px max, 22px gutter (16px on the phone); cards 14px radius, #E6E6E6 border, soft shadow.
  • Footer: “FeedSpark · Private & Confidential” left, page or version right.
  • Injected for you — never rebuilt: the live editor, presence avatars, the hours dot, Feed Chat, the phone bottom bar, migration badges, the ⓘ collapse.
  • Client documents (decks, exported PDF/HTML) follow the core deck template, not the app chrome, and never carry internal tools or wording.

Voice & copy

Write the way an AM talks to a client.

Do
  • “39 of 41 markets read · oldest 3 h ago”
  • “Not scanned” · “No AM on record”
  • “Ask the client to allowlist the scanner”
  • 24 Sep 2026 · £1,818.14 ex VAT
Don’t
  • “0 markets” when nothing was read
  • “Error: 403 upstream fetch failed”
  • A paragraph under every heading
  • 09/24/26 · £1.8k (on a quote)

UK English. One sentence visible; more collapses behind ⓘ. Name the source and the time of any figure. Money shows pence.

UX rules

Each one learnt from a real bug on the FCC.

One modal at a timeOpening one closes the others. A modal centres in the space the page leaves, never under a rail.
A flex row ignores hiddenSay [hidden]{display:none} once (fcc.css does), and test what is painted, not the property.
Nothing silentA failed save stays on screen and retries. A missing reading says so. A skipped row is counted, never dropped.
Undo, don’t just confirmDeletes are confirmed and can be put back; shared records keep who and when.
Shared work, personal screenThe work is shared by the team; a filter, a fold or a theme stays on the device.
Drafts, never sendsClient email lands as a Gmail draft for a person to send.
Esc closes the top thingPop-up before panel before full screen.
Reduced motion respectedDelight is optional; the information never is.
Deep linksA view worth sharing has a URL: ?client=&market=.

Never

From the brand source and the design review.

  • No FeedSpark logo inside content, no page-number circles.
  • No decorative lines or orange used as decoration.
  • No colour or font outside the tokens; no hex typed into a module.
  • No 500 / 600 weights; no second typeface.
  • No internal tools, weights or jargon on anything a client receives.
  • No figure without its source and time.

Build an AM module

The 30%: an AM’s own module on the FeedSpark look, automatically.

  1. Load the stylesheet — the brand, dark mode and components come with it.
  2. Use the tokens (var(--orange)), never a hex.
  3. Build from the components above: cards, KPI tiles, chips, tables, the source line.
  4. Read only approved, client-scoped data sources.
  5. Pass the checks every core page passes: module row, 390px phone, dark mode, security headers.
  6. Promotion to core is a reviewed pull request.
