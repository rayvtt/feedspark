How a rule is placed

By the field it writes first, then by what its name says it reads.

Stock controlWhat it coversRules in view

A custom label or an exclusion field carries no stock meaning on its own, so those rules count as stock rules only when the field or the name says stock, quantity, availability or range completion; a label named "New In" or "Clearance" is lifecycle. Channel comes from the field too — a fb_ or social_ field serves Meta, awin_ / olapic_ the affiliates, gb_ and pickup fields Google — and a base field such as stock_status serves every channel.
