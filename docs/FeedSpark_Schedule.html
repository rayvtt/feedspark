Scheduled work

The ASPL team's weekly optimisation schedule — Keywords, Titles, Product Types, Data tagging — is a stream of work that never enters Intake (project plans, calls, emails). Every week each client × market × task is offered with the hours left in the bucket, and the AM answers Go ahead or Skip. This reads the content team's sheet, hidden weekly tabs included, keeps the clients in your brand dossier in front, and counts how many months running each task has been skipped.

How the skip cadence is counted

  1. Source — the content team's Scheduled Title and Keyword Optimisation sheet. The visible Content-team-task-summary tab is one row per client × task × week since June 2025 (week in Dates, the AM's call in AM Status, column P). Before that, one hidden tab per task type per week ("KWs 1906", "Titles 0201" …) carries the AM's call in AM Confirmation and the ASPL outcome in Final Status; the week lives only in the tab name (DDMM), so its year is inferred from the first consolidated week.
  2. The AM's word — Skip / Cancelled / No / Not for this week count as skipped; Go ahead / Yes / OK / Approved as went ahead. AM Status outranks AM Confirmation; the ASPL Final Status stands in only when both are blank. Notes in brackets are not decisions.
  3. A skipped month — the task was on the schedule that month and the AM never said Go: every scheduled week was a skip. A month with no row at all is not scheduled — neither a skip nor a break in the streak (4-weekly cadences miss calendar months).
  4. The streak — consecutive decided months, counted back from the latest one, that were skipped. It ends at the first month that went ahead. Hrs skipped is the scheduled hours ("4 hours") of every skipped week inside the streak — the work the retainer was offered and did not take.
  5. Brands — sheet clients are matched to the FCC roster (brand dossier ∪ plan sheets ∪ wired feeds) by name, accents and spacing ignored ("Estee Lauder" → Estée Lauder, "All Saints" → AllSaints, "MAC Cosmetics" → MAC). Anything unmatched stays a sheet-only client behind whole sheet.
  6. Freshness — read live through the service account when the sheet is shared with it (cached 6 h, ⟳ re-reads now); otherwise the committed snapshot is served and says so. Nothing here writes to the sheet.
