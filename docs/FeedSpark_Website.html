01Why now

Shoppers ask AI what to buy. The answer is built from product data.

For twenty years a product feed had one job: it fed ads. Now it also feeds the assistants that answer a shopper's question before they visit a website at all, in Google's AI Mode and Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Meta's new shopping agent. They read what you send them.

A missing material or size used to make a weaker ad. Now it is a question the assistant can't answer about your product, so it recommends someone else's.

Sep 2026Google

Checkout inside AI Mode and Gemini for eligible US merchants. It reads Merchant Center price and availability, so a stale feed becomes a failed order rather than a disapproval.

Sep 2026Meta

A shopping agent that browses and buys for US shoppers, reading the retailer's own product record.

Sep 2026Google

Free product listings removed from Search across the EEA. Paid Shopping coverage now carries the weight in those markets.

2026Google

Conversational attributes join the product data specification (question_and_answer, related_product, document_link and more) so products can be found across AI-driven surfaces.

10Questions

Questions we get asked.

Our commerce platform already has a feed tool. Why a specialist?

A platform's feed tool exports what is in your store. A specialist changes what is in the feed: titles written for how people search, attributes filled from images and product pages, categories mapped properly, and every change tested. That is the difference between a feed that is approved and a feed that is chosen.

Do you replace our media agency?

No. We work alongside media agencies and in-house teams, and agencies are a large part of who we work with. Better product data makes every campaign they run perform better.

What makes a feed "AI-ready"?

Complete required data, clean identifiers, deep and accurate categories, rich detail such as material, pattern and highlights, and the conversational attributes Google publishes for AI surfaces. The free audit scores all of it.

Which channels do you support?

More than a hundred: Google, Microsoft, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, marketplaces, affiliates and price comparison sites, all from one source of truth in FeedHero.

Why price on a score rather than hours?

Because the score is what decides whether a product is shown. Hours measured our effort; the Golden Score and the AI-readiness scale measure your data, product by product, every day. You can see the number move, and so can we.

Can we still buy hours?

Yes. Project work outside the standard, such as scraping, image overlays or a channel launch, is quoted at our day rate.

How does switching to FeedSpark work?

We build and check your new feeds before anything is switched over, so campaigns keep running while you move. Your specialist handles the channel set-up with you.

Can we see our own data?

Yes. Clients log in to FeedHero to see their feeds, audits, batches and performance.
