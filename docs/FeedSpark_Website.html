Why feeds matter more in 2026

Search is becoming a conversation. Your feed is the answer.

For twenty years the product feed fed your ads. Now it feeds the assistants that decide what a shopper sees at all — and they don't browse your site the way a person does. They read the data you send them.

Assistants read data, not banners

AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT and Perplexity build shopping answers from structured product data. A missing material, size or colour isn't a weaker ad — it's a question the assistant can't answer, so it answers with someone else's product.

Checkout is moving into the answer

In the US, Google now offers checkout inside AI Mode and Gemini for eligible merchants — and it reads your Merchant Center price and availability. A stale feed stops being a disapproval and becomes a failed order.

Google, September 2026 · US, eligible merchants

Google now publishes attributes for AI

Conversational attributes — question_and_answer, related_product, document_link and more — exist so products can be discovered across AI-driven surfaces like AI Mode. They're optional, which is exactly why a catalogue that carries them stands out.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Anything else? hello@feedspark.com

Our commerce platform already has a feed tool. Why a specialist?

A platform's feed tool exports what's in your store. A specialist changes what's in the feed: titles written for how people search, attributes filled from images and product pages, categories mapped properly, and every change tested. That's the gap between a feed that's approved and a feed that's chosen.

Do you replace our media agency?

No. We work alongside media agencies and in-house teams — agencies are a large part of who we work with. Better product data makes every campaign they run perform better.

What makes a feed "AI-ready"?

Complete required data, clean identifiers, deep and accurate categories, rich detail — material, pattern, highlights, product detail — and the conversational attributes Google publishes for AI surfaces. Our free audit scores all of it across eight pillars.

Which channels do you support?

More than 100 — Google, Microsoft, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, marketplaces, affiliates and price comparison sites — all from one source of truth in FeedHero.

How does switching to FeedSpark work?

We build and validate your new feeds before anything is switched over, so your campaigns keep running while you move. Your specialist handles the channel set-up with you.

Can we see our own data?

Yes. Clients log in to FeedHero to see their feeds, audits, batches and performance — the same view our team works from.

The position

Every competitor we studied sells feeds as one part of something bigger — a commerce suite, a paid-media agency, an e-commerce platform. The one thing they can't claim is that feeds are the whole job. In 2026 that matters more than it did: AI assistants now build shopping answers straight from product data, so the feed has become the storefront. The page argues that in order — the shift, the proof, the specialist, the platform, the results.

What we took, and where we differ
