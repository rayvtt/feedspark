## 4. ⚠ The key-dates row is on 2024 dates

This is the most important thing in the file. The **day grid is 2026**, but **row 10 "UK Calendar
events and Key Dates" carries 2024 dates**. Every floating date I checked matches 2024 exactly:

| Event | Sheet says | 2024 | **2026 actual** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clocks go back | 27 Oct | 27 Oct ✓ | **25 Oct** |
| US Thanksgiving | 28 Nov | 28 Nov ✓ | **26 Nov** |
| **Black Friday** | 29 Nov | 29 Nov ✓ | **27 Nov** |
| Wear It Pink | 18 Oct | 18 Oct ✓ | **16 Oct** (3rd Fri) |
| Half term | 28 Oct – 1 Nov | matches 2024 | **w/c 26 Oct** (confirm by council) |
| **Diwali** | 31 Oct | 31 Oct/1 Nov ✓ | **8 November** |
| First day of autumn | 22 Sep *and* 23 Sep — the sheet contradicts itself in W3 vs W4 | | **23 Sep** |

Four out of four deterministic dates land on 2024. That is not coincidence — the row has been
carried over and not refreshed.

**Why it matters to us specifically:** we schedule each optimisation to be **live 21 days before**
its moment. Working off the sheet's dates would have put:

- the **Black Friday** optimisation live 8 Nov for a 29 Nov date — two days late against the real
  27 Nov, in the single highest-revenue window of the quarter;
- the **Diwali** optimisation live 10 Oct for a 31 Oct date — **a full week before the wrong date**,
  and eight days early against the real 8 Nov. The sheet also buckets Diwali with Halloween in the
  25–31 Oct week (R18 "HALLOWEEN DIWALI BEACH"), which in 2026 is simply the wrong week.

I have used the **corrected 2026 dates** in the import and split Diwali out of the Halloween week
into Sparkle Season on 8 Nov. Marked ✎ in the tables above.

**Recommended:** put the table above to the client before anything is briefed. It is a strong,
genuinely useful catch to lead a conversation with.

## 6. How to load it

1. Open `/kwcal`, select **Accessorize** in the brand selector (the importer files into the board
   you have open).
2. **✎ Edit calendar → ⤓ Import events**, paste `Accessorize_UK_kwcal_import.json`
   (or drop the file on the panel).
3. **⟳ Sync product types (live feed)** once — Accessorize GB is wired to its FeedHero XML.
4. On each moment, **⚙ Set scope** — the new picker browses the category tree, and
   **✨ Suggest from themes** will preselect product types matching the draft themes.
5. **→ Brief** files each one into the Workflow pipeline; from then on the status chip on the
   board is live from that ticket.

Duplicate protection is by *name + date*, so re-importing after edits is safe.
