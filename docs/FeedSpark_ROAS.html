How this reads

The quick breakdown behind every number on this page.
  1. Source. FeedHero's own ROAS dashboard (Google Ads), read via the FeedHero_reports MCP's roas_dashboard tool — the same figures a login at mcp.feedhero.net/reports/roas-dashboard would show. Impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue and spend come straight from Google Ads; SKUs, zombie% and the category split come from the product feed FeedHero has on file.
  2. Scope, deliberately narrow. FeedHero's client list carries 112 clients — most of them are not FeedSpark accounts. This page is hard-limited to the seven brands Ray named (Superdry, Reiss, Schuh, Monsoon, Accessorize, YuMOVE, Hobbycraft) across every market FeedHero has on file for them. A client outside that list is never pulled, never stored, never shown.
  3. Sync cadence. The worker pulls FeedHero itself, twice an hour (:10 and :40), 6 clients per firing, stalest-first — the ~57-market roster turns over roughly every 5 hours. FeedHero's own figures only move about once a day, so this is comfortably ahead of the source.
  4. Money is never summed across currencies. A brand's markets can report in different currencies (Superdry alone spans GBP, EUR and USD) — every spend/revenue figure here is kept apart by its own symbol, and a "blended ROAS%" is only shown where a brand's read markets share exactly one currency.
  5. The band is FeedHero's own verdict — Strong 400%+ ROAS, Steady 200%+, Weak 100%+, Losing under that — not a threshold invented here. A market with no Google Ads activity in the window carries no band at all, which is a different fact from Losing.
  6. Zombie% is FeedHero's own read of SKUs carrying zero impressions or clicks in the window — catalogue sitting in the feed with no Shopping visibility at all.
  7. This is v1. A working surface for the raw numbers — the next step is reading a category's ROAS band alongside its Golden Record / content-quality score on the same brand, so a Losing category and a weak feed can be shown as one finding rather than two separate pages.
