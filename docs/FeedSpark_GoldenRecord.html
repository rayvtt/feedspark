The Golden Record — live attribute completeness

Every wired Google Shopping feed, scored live against Google’s product data specification: which attributes are required for every product, which are required in specific cases (apparel, variants, identifiers), and which are recommended — the optimisation surface where FeedSpark does its work. Coverage is captured on every estate scan at zero extra cost, the Golden Record score trends per feed, and a coverage drop on a required attribute flags here and badges every FCC page — before products start disapproving. An intentional change? “Expected — accept as known-good”.
